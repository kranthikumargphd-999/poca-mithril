About SecureAI

Building Trust in
AI Interactions

SecureAI is a pioneering platform designed to address the critical challenge of trust and verification in AI systems. By leveraging Django for web infrastructure, Ganache for blockchain simulation, and innovative protocols like PoCA (Proof-of-Context Attestation) and Mithril signature aggregation, we provide a comprehensive solution for verifiable AI interactions.

Our platform ensures that every AI response is cryptographically secured, timestamped, and stored on the blockchain, making tampering virtually impossible. This creates an immutable audit trail that builds trust between AI systems and their users.

Three-Layer Architecture

Building trust through a multi-layered approach

1

Django Web Application

User authentication, AI agent registration, request submission, and comprehensive dashboard management with SQLite database.

2

PoCA & Mithril Engine

Cryptographic proof generation, signature aggregation, Merkle tree construction, and batch processing for efficient verification.

3

Blockchain Layer

Ganache local blockchain with Solidity smart contracts storing hashes, signatures, and Merkle roots for tamper-proof verification.
